# Glossary

- **$\mathrm{AME}(N, d)$ (Absolutely Maximally Entangled State):** A pure $N$-party state in $(\mathbb{C}^d)^{\otimes N}$ whose every $\lfloor N/2 \rfloor$-party reduced density matrix is maximally mixed.
- **Canonical $\mathbb{Z}_d$ Graph State:** A stabilizer state defined by a symmetric zero-diagonal adjacency matrix $\Gamma \in \mathrm{Sym}_N(\mathbb{Z}_d)$ via generators $g_j = X_j \prod_{l} Z_l^{\Gamma_{jl}}$.
- **Cyclic Weyl–Heisenberg $\mathbb{Z}_d$-Stabilizer Subgroup:** An isotropic subgroup $S \le \mathbb{Z}_d^{2N}$ (under $\omega_d((x,z),(x',z')) = \sum_j (x_j z'_j - z_j x'_j) \pmod d$) of order $d^N$.
- **Pless Power Moments:** Pure-integer combinatorial double-counting identities relating the weight enumerator $A_w$ of a $k$-uniform code/stabilizer to binomial subset projections.
- **Rains Shadow Enumerator ($S_j$):** Non-negative integer weight distribution of the shadow coset $S + c$ where $(-1)^{\operatorname{wt}(u)} = (-1)^{\langle c, u \rangle}$ on an isotropic binary subgroup $S \le \mathbb{F}_2^{2N}$.
- **$t$-$\mathrm{RQLS}(v)$ (Resolvable Quantum Latin Square):** A $v \times v$ array of unit vectors in $\mathbb{C}^v$ (each row and column an orthonormal basis) admitting $t$ mutually orthogonal classical transversal resolutions; **maximal cardinality** means all $v^2$ rays are pairwise distinct ($c = v^2$, i.e. $|\langle \psi_{ij} | \psi_{i'j'} \rangle| < 1$ for $(i,j) \neq (i',j')$).
- **$\mathrm{STAB}_n$ / $\Lambda_n$:** The $n$-qudit stabilizer polytope and its polar dual $\Lambda_n = \{X \in \mathrm{Herm}_1 : \operatorname{Tr}(X |\psi\rangle\langle\psi|) \ge 0\ \forall |\psi\rangle \in \mathcal{S}_n\}$.
- **$\mathrm{ASTAB}$ / $\mathrm{AWP}$ / $\mathrm{ASEP}$:** Sets of density matrices remaining stabilizer, Wigner-positive, or separable under ALL global unitary conjugations $U \rho U^\dagger$.
- **QGTL (Quantum Geometric Tensor):** $Q_{\mu\nu} = \langle \partial_\mu \psi | (I - |\psi\rangle\langle\psi|) | \partial_\nu \psi \rangle = g_{\mu\nu} - \frac{i}{2} \Omega_{\mu\nu}$, combining the Fubini–Study metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ and Berry curvature $\Omega_{\mu\nu}$.
