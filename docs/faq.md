**Q1: Does Theorem `T1e` contradict the existence of stabilizer $\mathrm{AME}(4, 4)$?**
No! The famous stabilizer $\mathrm{AME}(4, 4)$ is constructed over the **non-cyclic Galois field** $\mathbb{F}_4 = \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ (whose Pauli group is $\mathbb{F}_2^2$-indexed, exponent 2). Theorem `T1e` proves that NO **cyclic Weyl–Heisenberg $\mathbb{Z}_4$-stabilizer** subgroup $S \le \mathbb{Z}_4^8$ of order $4^4 = 256$ (whether canonical graph state, free, or non-free torsion) can define $\mathrm{AME}(4, 4)$ — and we explicitly exhibit the $\mathbb{F}_4$-stabilizer witness alongside the $\mathbb{Z}_4$ impossibility proof (Ring-vs-Field Separation).

**Q2: How does Pillar II relate to Ball & Simoens (`arXiv:2607.12933v2`) and Zhang & Cao (`arXiv:2608.29201v2`)?**
