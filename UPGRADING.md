# Upgrading

This is the initial `v1.0.0` release of `quantum-phasespace-rigidity` (`0TH-2026-020`).

- All Lean theorem names exported in `PalomarChallenge.lean` and `comparator.json` are frozen under semantic versioning (`1.x.y`).
- Any future theorem strengthening or errata will bump minor (`1.1.0`) or patch (`1.0.1`) versions and record exact migration notes here and in `ERRATA.md`.
